Staff


Staff at Warpcon help ensure events run smoothly, and are a vital part of the Warpcon team.


Staff are volunteers, and are not paid for their work, however; they do gain access to the event for free and will receive an exclusive Staff Warpcon T-Shirt. Staff are also eligible for complementary lunch on days they are working.








Traders
